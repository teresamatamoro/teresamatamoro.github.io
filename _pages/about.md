## About me

I am a third year PhD student at the University of Bristol working under the supervision of [Dr. Sotiria Fotopoulou]([(https://www.sotiriafotopoulou.com)]), working in the Data-Intensive Astronomical Analysis (D.I.AN.A.). My research focuses on Active Galactic Nuclei and their co‑evolution with host galaxies, combining multi‑wavelength datasets with modern machine‑learning techniques. I am also an active member of the Euclid consortium and serve as deputy co‑lead of the Photometry and Catalogues tiger team within the AGN and Galaxy Evolution working group.

My work includes building multi‑band photometric catalogues for AGN identification and parameter estimation, as well as forward‑modelling galaxy and AGN spectra to explore differences between cosmological simulations and real observations using machine learning. Broadly, I’m interested in how AGN shape their host galaxies and how data‑driven methods can reveal the physical processes behind this connection.
